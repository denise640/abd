How to walk it yourself

Safe: this view is read only, sends nothing to the tenant, and ends after 30 minutes.

  1. Go to Leasing → Test portal (/admin/leasing/test-portal). Admin access needed.
  2. Pick any current resident. "Not invited yet" is fine.
  3. Switch between Computer and Phone frames at the top.
  4. Click through each page on the map above. Buttons that would save something stay visible but are refused. That is expected, not a bug.
  5. Anything else that looks wrong: file it on Business Management → Bug Hunting.
  6. Click Sign out in the frame when done.

Known test portal issues, already logged: no "View their portal" link from the resident record, a resident with no email shows a red error, and the address bar always reads /tenant/home.
